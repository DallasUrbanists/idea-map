# Open MVP beta participant notice policy

The application displays these notices accessibly before a beta submission and
links to the applicable [beta Terms of Use](beta-terms-of-use-policy.md) and
[Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md). These notices do not replace privacy,
consent, removal or reliability safeguards.

The following contents links navigate within this guide:

- [Splash or welcome notice](#splash-or-welcome-notice)
- [Submission context](#submission-context)
- [Existing rights and terms agreement](#existing-rights-and-terms-agreement)
- [Notice requirements](#notice-requirements)

## Splash or welcome notice

Idea Map is an early public beta. Features may change, and bugs or interruptions
may occur. Posts appear publicly immediately and are not reviewed before
publication. Share only content you have permission to publish, and don't
include sensitive personal information. Please report problems or request
removal at contact@dallasurbanists.org.

## Submission context

Your submitted text, selected location, photos and captions will be public
immediately. Saved drafts stay private. Don't include private contact details
or other sensitive personal information in the public fields.

## Existing rights and terms agreement

Keep the per-submission checkbox unchecked by default, linked to the exact
accepted terms version:

“I agree to the Idea Map Terms of Use and confirm that I have the rights needed
to submit this text and these photos.”

The public-posting notice does not replace this agreement or grant consent for
existing private submissions. The form and server must retain the exact text,
version and acceptance timestamp.

## Notice requirements

The submission notice must match the public fields submitted by the form, and
an assigned operator must monitor the report and removal inbox. The notices
must not claim guaranteed security, reliability or data protection, or waive
privacy responsibilities. Maintainers should record the disposition of the
[independent terms and privacy review](https://github.com/DallasUrbanists/idea-map/issues/212)
and any conditions before accepting beta submissions.
