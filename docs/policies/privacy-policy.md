# Privacy Policy

You can find out here what information Idea Map uses, why it's needed, and how
to request a correction or removal. Dallas Urbanists operates Idea Map, and you
can send privacy questions and requests to
[contact@dallasurbanists.org](mailto:contact@dallasurbanists.org).

## Information and purposes

Idea Map handles account identifiers, sign-in details, email verification
status, submission contact information, text, photos, captions, selected
locations, and consent records. These support accounts, ownership verification,
submissions, moderation, publication, email delivery, and recovery from service
failures.

Idea Map uses session cookies and browser storage to maintain sign-in, drafts,
saved choices, and preferences. Data saved in your browser stays in that
browser's storage. Clearing it can remove drafts and preferences. Signing out
clears that browser's session and leaves your other devices signed in.

## Public and private information

Approved ideas and permitted media are public and can be viewed or copied by
other people. Pending submissions and edits stay private until approved. If you
edit a published idea, the approved version stays visible until the edit is
approved.

Contact information, ownership evidence, private media, moderation notes, and
restricted review records remain private. Staff can access information only when
they need it for authorized work. Idea Map does not include private contact
details in public attribution.

## Service providers

Dallas Urbanists uses service providers for hosting, authentication, database
and media storage, transactional email, maps, and content assessment. Providers
receive only the information needed for the service they perform on its behalf;
public display does not grant providers independent reuse rights in submissions.

Map and content-assessment features can involve external processing of selected
locations or submitted content. Idea Map reviews provider data handling, access,
retention, and regional terms before sending private information. Optional
processing remains disabled when its use and data handling are unresolved.

## Retention and deletion

Dallas Urbanists verifies a requester's authority before removing content. Once
removal is authorized, Idea Map stops public access immediately, including
media, previews, and cached copies it controls. Primary stored copies are
deleted within 30 days, and backup copies expire within 90 days of that same
authorized removal.

Documented legal holds can require retention beyond those limits. Retained
copies have restricted access, and restoring a backup does not make removed
content public again. Removal does not erase copies already downloaded by other
people.

Idea Map keeps metadata about moderation decisions for 90 days. Trust-history
records are maintained separately so metadata expiration does not alter approval
counts; corrections to moderation decisions update the history used for later
decisions. Raw content, contact details, credentials, and full provider
responses do not belong in operational logs.

Account data and uploads have purpose-specific retention rules. Exact periods
outside authorized content removal and moderation metadata, and response-time
targets for incoming requests, remain unresolved.

## Your choices and requests

You can choose what to submit, decline to agree to a submission, sign out, clear
data saved in your browser, or contact Dallas Urbanists about access,
correction, and removal. Clearing browser storage does not remove a submission
held on the server. You can request removal to address those stored copies.

Dallas Urbanists checks requests without exposing another person's private
information. The [Terms of Use](terms-of-use-policy.md) explain ownership and
the limited service license for contributions.
