# Proposed beta participant notice

This is review copy, not an accepted legal disclaimer or a claim that the beta
is live. The application should display the accepted version accessibly before
submission and link to the matching Terms and Privacy Policy.

## Splash or welcome notice

Idea Map is an early public beta. Features may change, and bugs or interruptions
may occur. Posts appear publicly immediately and are not reviewed before
publication. Share only content you have permission to publish, and don't
include sensitive personal information. Please report problems or request
removal at contact@dallasurbanists.org.

## Submission context

Your submitted text, selected location, photos and captions will be public
immediately. Saved drafts stay private. Don't include private contact details
or other sensitive personal information in the public fields.

## Existing rights and terms agreement

Keep the per-submission checkbox unchecked by default, linked to the exact
accepted terms version:

“I agree to the Idea Map Terms of Use and confirm that I have the rights needed
to submit this text and these photos.”

The public-posting notice does not replace this agreement or grant consent for
existing private submissions. The form and server must retain the exact text,
version and acceptance timestamp.
