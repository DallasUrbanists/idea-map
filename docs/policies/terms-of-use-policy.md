## Your account and submissions

You should protect your account credentials and submit accurate information
about your authority to share content. You must have the rights needed to submit
your text and photos; material without those rights cannot be accepted for
publication. Account registration does not replace agreement to these terms for
a submission.

You must explicitly agree to these terms each time you submit an idea, whether you use an
account or submit as a guest. The agreement box starts unchecked and links to
the exact terms that apply. Claiming a guest submission does not replace its
original agreement.

You retain ownership of the text and photos you submit. You grant Dallas

moderate, display, and distribute your contribution only as needed to operate
Idea Map, including creating accessible or technically compatible versions.

Dallas Urbanists can use hosting and moderation providers to perform those tasks
on its behalf. That permission does not authorize providers or the public to
independently reuse your contribution. The service license applies while content
is retained for service use, including the limited backup retention in the
[Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md#retention-and-deletion).

You should identify required attribution for material you submit. Idea Map
displays that attribution where appropriate without exposing private contact
details. Public display does not put submissions under the software's Apache-2.0
license or grant the public a general reuse license.

## Acceptable use and moderation

You should share good-faith civic ideas and respect other people. Idea Map does
not publish credible threats, incitement, targeted harassment, hateful attacks,
explicit sexual material, unauthorized private information, spam, fraud,
malware, or material you lack permission to share.

Idea Map reviews submissions and edits before publication. Moderators can
approve, reject, request changes, hold a submission, or remove published
material under the [moderation policy](moderation-policy.md). Good-faith
criticism, political views, and disagreement alone are not grounds for
rejection.

You can correct a submission or request another review of a moderation decision.
An appeal does not make rejected content public. Rights, privacy, removal, and
appeal questions can be sent to
[contact@dallasurbanists.org](mailto:contact@dallasurbanists.org).

## Removal and privacy

You can request removal of your content. Dallas Urbanists verifies your
authority and handles access, retention, and deletion under the
[Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md#retention-and-deletion). Removal cannot
recall copies already obtained by others.

External services have their own terms for direct use. Displaying a map, linked
design, or third-party material does not grant additional rights in that
material. Idea Map content is civic discussion, not a guarantee of accuracy,
official approval, or safe navigation. Users should verify information before
acting on it.

## Changes to these terms

Idea Map identifies the terms version accepted with each submission and retains
that version for review. Updated terms apply when you explicitly agree to them
with a submission. Changing the terms does not create consent for older content.
