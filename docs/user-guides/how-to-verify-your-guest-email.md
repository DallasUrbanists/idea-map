# Verify your guest email

You can use a guest verification link to confirm the email address associated
with your submission, without creating an account or signing in.

The following contents links navigate within this guide:

- [Before you begin](#before-you-begin)
- [Confirm your email](#confirm-your-email)
- [Handle a link that does not work](#handle-a-link-that-does-not-work)

## Before you begin

You need a complete, unused verification link for your own submission and a
browser with JavaScript enabled. Verification must be available on the site you
are using; opening the page alone does not establish that the service is
available. This guide starts with a link you already have: the verification page
has no control for sending or replacing links.

You should keep the link private. Anyone with the link can use it to confirm the
associated email address. Successful confirmation records that the email is
verified and uses up the link. It does not sign you in, grant ownership or editing
access, or publish your submission.

## Confirm your email

You can confirm your email by following these steps:

1. Open the complete verification link to reach **Verify your email**.
2. Select **Verify email** when you are ready to confirm the address for your
   submission.
3. Wait for “Your email has been verified.” before closing the page.

Opening the link does not confirm your email until you select **Verify email**.
The page removes the private part of the link from the address bar as it opens.
You should keep the original link until you finish; copying the address bar or
refreshing the page does not preserve the link needed for confirmation.

## Handle a link that does not work

You can use the message on the page to understand the result:

| Message | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| “JavaScript is required to use this verification link.” | Enable JavaScript for the site in your browser, then reopen the original link. |
| “This link is not valid. Request another verification link.” | If you refreshed the page or copied its address after opening it, reopen the complete original link. If that link also fails, you need another link. |
| “This link is invalid, expired, or already used. Request another link.” | Use another valid link; this message does not identify which condition applies. A link cannot be used again after successful verification. |
| “Verification could not be completed. Request another link.” | Verification is not confirmed; the service might be unavailable. The page cannot provide a replacement link. |
| “Verification could not be confirmed. Request another link.” | Do not assume verification succeeded or failed; a connection problem can prevent the result from reaching you. The page cannot check the result or provide a replacement link. |
| “Wait for the current request to finish, then reopen the link.” | Wait for the in-progress request to finish, then reopen the complete link you tried to open while it was running. |

The page's request-another-link messages do not open a request form or send an
email. If you need a replacement and have no supported way to obtain one, you
cannot finish verification through this page. You should not share your private
link in a public comment or support request.
