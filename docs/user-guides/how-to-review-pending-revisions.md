# Review pending revisions

As a moderator, you can inspect pending submissions and edits, place or release
holds, and request or resolve changes. A revision is one submitted version of
an idea. These actions record your review without approving or rejecting it.

The following contents links navigate within this guide:

- [Before you begin](#before-you-begin)
- [Open a revision](#open-a-revision)
- [Record a review action](#record-a-review-action)
- [Recover from an error](#recover-from-an-error)

## Before you begin

You need a signed-in, verified account with current moderator access, and the
moderation screen must be available in the environment you use. If you do not
have access or the screen is unavailable, contact the person coordinating your
moderation work rather than trying a different account to bypass the restriction.

The screen does not display private media and offers no approval or rejection
controls. You should not treat the displayed text as a complete review of a
submission that includes media. You should follow the
[moderation policy](../policies/moderation-policy.md) and keep unpublished
content and review notes private.

## Open a revision

You can open a pending revision with these steps:

1. Open the moderation page at `/moderation` on the Idea Map site you use.
2. Select **Sign in or manage your account** if you need to sign in, then return
   to the moderation page.
3. Select **Refresh queue** to load the current page of **Pending revisions**.
4. Select **Review revision** followed by the revision identifier you want to
   inspect, or select **Next page** if it appears and you need more results.
5. Read **Submitted content**, **Active holds**, and **Active change requests**
   before choosing an action.

The revision shows its version and review state. If the page says **No pending
revisions on this page**, there is nothing to open on that page; you can refresh
later. Submitted content may include labels and punctuation around the text.

## Record a review action

Your action and reason become part of the private review record. You should keep
contact details and submitted content out of the reason and describe only why
the action is needed. Removing a hold or resolving a change request removes that
particular block; other blocks can remain, and the action does not itself approve
the revision.

You can choose among these actions:

| Action | When to use it |
| --- | --- |
| **Place a hold** | Pause publication while a concern needs review. |
| **Request changes** | Record a change that needs to be addressed before publication. |
| **Release a hold** | Remove a selected active hold after its concern is addressed. |
| **Resolve a change request** | Close a selected active change request after checking it is addressed. |

You can record the selected action with these steps:

1. Choose the action in **Action**.
2. For **Release a hold** or **Resolve a change request**, select the matching
   item in **Hold to release** or **Change request to resolve**.
3. Enter a brief, single-line **Reason** without tabs or other control characters.
   The limit is 1,000 bytes, so accented letters and other characters can use more
   than one unit of that limit.
4. Select **Record action** once and wait for the result.
5. When **Action recorded** appears, reopen the revision through **Review revision**
   to inspect its current state before taking another action.

## Recover from an error

If a reason needs correction, the screen keeps the open review, selected action,
and your text. You can remove line breaks or unsupported characters, shorten the
reason if needed, and select **Record action** again.

If the screen says **This review changed**, you should refresh the queue and
reopen the revision. You should read the new state before deciding whether the
same action is still appropriate. The old review and reason are cleared to
prevent acting on an outdated view.

If the screen says **Review is unavailable**, you should wait and refresh the
queue, then reopen the revision before retrying. A failed response does not prove
that an action was not recorded; you should check the active holds and change
requests before repeating it. If the problem continues, contact the person
coordinating your moderation work without sharing submitted content publicly.

If the screen asks you to sign in, you can use **Sign in or manage your account**
and then return and refresh. If your account no longer has moderator access,
you should contact the person coordinating your moderation work. A revision
that is **no longer awaiting review** cannot be opened here; you can refresh the
queue to see the remaining revisions.
