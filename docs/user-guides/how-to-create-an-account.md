# Create an account

You can create an account with your email address and password, then verify your
email address using the link sent to you. You need access to that email inbox.

1. Open Idea Map and select **Account**.
2. Enter your email address and a password.
3. Select **Create account**. The account page shows that you are signed in and
    asks you to check your email.
4. Open the verification email and follow its link.
5. Return to the account page and reload it to update your verification status.

If you cannot find the email, check your spam folder. To request another link,
select **Send verification email** on the account page and use the newest email.
If account creation reports an error but the page shows you are signed in,
request a verification email there. Otherwise, check your details and try again.
If your session could not be refreshed, check your connection and wait for the
automatic retry, or sign out and sign in again.

You can also [sign in with Google](how-to-sign-in.md) from the account page.
