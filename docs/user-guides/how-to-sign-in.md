# Sign in

You can sign in to your Idea Map account with your email and password or with
Google. Your sign-in persists across visits in the same browser.

1. Open Idea Map and select **Account**.
2. Enter your email address and password, then select **Sign in**. To use
    Google instead, select **Continue with Google** and complete the prompt.
3. Check that the account page shows your signed-in email address.

If sign-in fails, check your details and try again. If you forgot your password,
you can [reset it](how-to-reset-your-password.md). If the page says Google
sign-in is unavailable, try again when it becomes available.

If the page says your session could not be refreshed, check your connection and
wait for the automatic retry. You can also sign out and sign in again.
