# Sign out

You can sign out of Idea Map on the browser you are using. This does not sign
you out on your other devices or browsers.

1. Open Idea Map and select **Account**.
2. Select **Sign out on this device**.
3. Check that the sign-in controls appear and the page confirms you signed out.

If sign-out fails, check your connection and reload the page. If the account
page still shows you are signed in, select **Sign out on this device** again.
To end a session in another browser, open Idea Map there and sign out in that
browser too.
