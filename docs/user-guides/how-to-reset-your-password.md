# Reset your password

You can request a password reset if you cannot remember your Idea Map password.
You need access to the email address associated with your Idea Map account.

1. Open Idea Map and select **Account**.
2. If you are signed in, select **Sign out on this device**.
3. Enter your account email address and select **Reset password**.
4. Check your email and follow the reset link to choose a new password.
5. Return to the account page and sign in with your new password.

The confirmation does not indicate whether an account exists for the address.
If the email does not arrive, check the address and your spam folder, then
request another reset link.
